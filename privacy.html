Your Privacy Matters

This Privacy Policy explains what information My To-Do List may collect, how it is used, and how we work to protect your information.

01

Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, My To-Do List may collect information that you provide when creating or using your account.

  • Name or username
  • Email address
  • Account information
  • Tasks and reminders you create
  • Family member information you choose to add
02

How We Use Your Information

Information collected by My To-Do List may be used to provide and improve the features of the app.

  • Provide access to your account
  • Save and display your tasks
  • Provide reminders and notifications
  • Provide family features
  • Maintain and improve the app
  • Help troubleshoot problems
03

Account Information

If you create an account, information associated with your account may be stored so that you can sign in and access your app data across supported devices.

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials secure.

04

Tasks and Reminders

Tasks, reminders, and related information that you create may be stored so that the app can display and manage them.

Reminder notifications may use information about your tasks and reminder settings to provide the requested notifications.

05

Family Information

If you use family features, you may choose to provide information about family members or other people you add.

This information may include names, email addresses, and roles. You should only provide information that you have permission to provide.

06

Third-Party Services

My To-Do List may use third-party services to provide certain features, including authentication, database storage, and other app functionality.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

07

Notifications

My To-Do List may send notifications for reminders, overdue tasks, and other app activity when notifications are enabled on your device.

You can control notification permissions through your device settings.

08

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to help protect information used by My To-Do List.

However, no online service or electronic storage system can guarantee complete security.

09

Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the app's features, maintain accounts, provide backups, improve services, or meet applicable requirements.

Deleted information may remain temporarily available when needed for features such as deleted-task recovery or other app functionality.

10

Your Choices

Depending on the features available, you may be able to update or delete certain information associated with your account.

You can also control certain permissions, including notifications, through your device settings.

11

Children's Privacy

My To-Do List is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that personal information has been provided by a child in a way that violates applicable requirements, please contact support.

12

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app changes, new features are introduced, or privacy practices change.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

13

Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled, please contact the My To-Do List support team.

Email: support@My To-Do List.com

Important

This Privacy Policy is intended to describe the general privacy practices of My To-Do List. Make sure the final policy accurately matches the information your app actually collects, the services it uses, and the legal requirements that apply to your app.

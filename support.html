Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a task?

Open My To-Do List and use the task creation option to add a new task. Enter the information for your task and save it.

How do reminders work?

Reminders can notify you when a task is due or when a task becomes overdue. Make sure notifications are enabled for My To-Do List in your device settings.

Can I delete a task?

Yes. Tasks can be deleted from your task list. Deleted tasks may temporarily appear in the deleted tasks section before being permanently removed.

Can I add family members?

Yes. My To-Do List can include family features that allow you to add family members with information such as their name, email address, and role.

What if I still need help?

If you cannot find an answer here, contact support and provide as much information as possible about the issue you're having.
